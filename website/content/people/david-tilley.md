### Background

Following a first postdoctoral stay at Princeton University, David Tilley moved to Switzerland to learn about water splitting with Prof. Michael Grätzel at EPFL. Following this postdoctoral fellowship, he served as Group Leader for the water splitting subgroup in the Grätzel laboratory from 2011-2014. He was appointed as an Assistant Professor in the Department of Chemistry of the University of Zurich (UZH) in 2015 and was promoted to Associate Professor in 2020. In August 2024, he became director of the Department of Chemistry at UZH.
